Geon Deterministic Orbit Integrator

Fluid-Dynamic Trajectory Tracking:

Instead of randomly scattering points based on the Schrödinger probability distribution (Monte Carlo), this engine drops a single particle into the mathematical Casimir/Centrifugal equilibrium bowl.

Using Euler integration, the continuous continuous fluid-dynamic forces (Fr, Fθ, Fφ) drive the particle in real-time to organically weave the dense 3D orbital shape over thousands of frames.
