# Core Theoretical Principles of the Geon Fluid-Dynamic Framework

## 1. Redefining the Fields (Strain vs. Momentum)
In a hyper-elastic tensor fluid (Casimir Vacuum), light is a mechanical shear wave.
*   **The Electric Field (E):** Spatial Strain. It represents how far the vacuum fluid has been physically stretched, twisted, or displaced from its neutral, high-pressure resting state. It is structural tension.
*   **The Magnetic Field (B):** Temporal Momentum (Vorticity). It represents the kinetic velocity of the vacuum fluid as it violently snaps back to try and heal the structural tension.

## 2. The Engine of the Derivative
The wave propagates because the medium cannot tolerate a steep gradient. The energy oscillates continuously between pure geometry (strain) and pure time (motion) via the spatial derivative.

*   $\nabla\times E$ is the Spatial Derivative (the steepness of the twist in the fluid).
*   $-\frac{\partial B}{\partial t}$ is the Temporal Acceleration of the fluid snapping back.

**The Compression/Decompression Cycle:**
1.  The vacuum fluid is stretched to its absolute maximum limit (Peak E). The fluid is highly decompressed (under extreme tension).
2.  Because the fluid is perfectly elastic, a steep spatial gradient ($\nabla$) exists. The fluid cannot tolerate this gradient.
3.  The tension collapses, converting the stored structural energy entirely into kinetic velocity (Peak B).
4.  The fluid's momentum overshoots the neutral baseline, twisting the vacuum in the exact opposite direction, creating a new zone of extreme compression.

## 3. The Propagation of the "Droplet" (Photon)
*   The photon is not an object; it is a self-cannibalizing gradient.
*   It oscillates continuously between Potential Energy (structural limit, E) and Kinetic Energy (momentum crashing back, B).
*   Because the vacuum has a specific kinematic viscosity and density, this overshoot-and-collapse mechanism cascades forward at terminal velocity $c$.

## 4. The Geon Connection (Electron & Proton)
*   Elementary particles are closed loops of this traveling compression/decompression wave.
*   When forced to bite its own tail, the derivative ($\nabla\times E$) is locked into a permanent, standing geometric twist.
*   The fluid is permanently trying to snap back (B), but its forward momentum is perfectly trapped by the $4\pi$ topology (or Trefoil).
*   The fields stop propagating outward and instead generate a permanent, localized outward pressure against the Casimir vacuum.
